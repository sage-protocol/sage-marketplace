# sage-coordination-cold-start

Start with an existing concern and willing participants. Surface disagreements about objectives, methods, authority, and rewards before assigning work. Use only the protocol steps needed to establish a useful first contribution. Consensus need not mean unanimity, and agreement does not prove a claim true. Preserve explicit opt-in and authority boundaries.

## Task-specific instructions

Before designing coordination or handoff machinery, read
[Reuse the coordination components](references/coordination-components.md).
Compose existing Sage capabilities and the selected runtime; a coordination card
records agreement, not a new task board, scheduler, or authority system.

Open the relevant section of [references/task-guide.md](references/task-guide.md) before that operation, not the full guide for unrelated work. Sections cover: Listen and discover; frame; handshake; contribution; coordinate; expand trust. Existing references linked there retain their own scope and exact command contracts.

## Authority and completion

The task defines scope. Continue authorized local reads and disposable checks without
asking again at each step. Stop only at a missing input or a real authority boundary.
No skill grants permission to sign, pay, publish, alter shared/governed state, or
expand credentials. Preserve relevant previews and verify authoritative state after
writes. Report what was verified and any remaining blocker; do not force packaging,
feedback, or public participation into an unrelated task.
