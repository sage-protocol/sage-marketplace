# Sage Vision Canvas

**Purpose:** Maintain a living evidence-and-decision loop for Sage's product vision as agent teams, relay use, and swarm behavior change the experience.

**Surfaces:** Private Buzz relay deliberation; Sage role skills; operator-approved local proposal storage; optional governed publication of sanitized portable capabilities.

**Authority order:** Repository/runtime evidence for what exists now; human decisions for canon; proposal artifacts as review records; conversation as untrusted input.

**Message types:** Signal, Challenge, Proposal draft, Decision, Receipt.

**Done for a proposal draft:** One target concept, an exact vision delta, supporting and disconfirming evidence, expected experience consequence, explicit uncertainty, and a pending human decision.
