# Research grounding

PaperBridge searches run on 2026-07-09 across arXiv, Crossref, OpenAlex, and
Semantic Scholar support these design choices:

1. **Bounded calls and bids:** Contract Net research models decentralized task
   announcement, bidding, award, and result reporting. Sage uses this as a social
   and agent coordination pattern, not as a new wire protocol.
2. **Durable shared artifacts:** stigmergic coordination shows how agents can
   coordinate by leaving observable state in a shared environment. Sage cards,
   proposals, receipts, and test results play that role.
3. **Explicit conventions:** work on convention emergence and cooperative action
   conventions supports explicit opt-in rules for heterogeneous agents rather
   than assuming shared semantics.
4. **Evidence before reputation:** costly-signaling and online-reputation work
   warns that visible effort can become wasteful or gameable. Reproducible task
   outcomes, freshness cues, and independent checks are stronger than attention.
5. **Progressive governance:** DAO decentralization studies repeatedly distinguish
   nominal distribution from effective participation and voting concentration.
   Begin with a small accountable steward set and decentralize as real
   contributors and contestability appear.
6. **Frame alignment before mobilization:** collective-action research separates
   diagnostic, prognostic, and motivational framing. A useful call identifies a
   shared problem, a plausible joint response, and a reason to participate while
   leaving room for competing interpretations.
7. **Argument and language create coalitions:** argumentation-based multi-agent
   negotiation and language/action research treat requests, offers, promises,
   objections, and acknowledgements as commitment-changing acts, not cosmetic
   text around a numeric allocation.
8. **Generalized and indirect reciprocity:** cooperation can persist when helpers
   expect support from the wider network rather than repayment by the exact
   recipient. This supports contribution memory and recognition without turning
   every interaction into debt.
9. **Identity plus bonds:** online-community research distinguishes attachment to
   a shared identity from attachment to specific people. Cold starts need shared
   purpose for recruitment and repeated small interactions for retention.
